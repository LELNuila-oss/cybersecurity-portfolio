# IdentityGuard roadmap and gates

**Status:** planned. Phase 0 only has authorization to proceed.

| Phase | Objective | Deliverable | Exit gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Market & problem definition | Findings, problem definition, traceability, MVP scope | Owner validates focus and understanding |
| 1 | Business case and governance requirements | Fictional 300-person company, AS-IS/TO-BE, stakeholders, risks, KPI baseline | Owner approves requirements |
| 2 | Architecture and data model | Identity/account/role/entitlement/app model, ADRs | Owner reviews design |
| 3 | Identity core and RBAC | Synthetic people, roles, policies | Tests and owner approval |
| 4 | JML lifecycle | Provision/change/revoke processes | Positive/negative tests |
| 5 | Requests, approvals and reviews | Approval and certification workflows | Traceable evidence |
| 6 | Identity risk and SoD | Orphaned/excess access and SoD detection | Risk-control tests |
| 7 | Enterprise-style integrations | REST/SCIM and optional IdP integration | Integration tests |
| 8 | Auditability and monitoring | Logs, exceptions, metrics, risk dashboard | Audit reconstruction tests |
| 9 | Security and resilience | Failure/recovery, authz and boundary tests | Test evidence and remediation |
| 10 | Business results and career portfolio | Measured lab outcomes, demo, CV/LinkedIn explanation | Owner walkthrough |

**Do not proceed to Phase 1 without explicit approval.** The time estimate is provisional and depends on available study hours.
