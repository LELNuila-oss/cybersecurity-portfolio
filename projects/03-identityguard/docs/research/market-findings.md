## Selected employers and business signals

- **RSM US (historical Senior Associate, IAM/SailPoint, San Salvador):** lifecycle, provisioning, approvals, governance/certification, integrations and client communication. Senior role; not an entry-level listing.
- **Medtronic (historical Identity Lifecycle & Governance):** employee lifecycle, reviews, role management, source and target integrations; the report describes related academic/project experience as potentially relevant.
- **Cognizant (historical Senior SailPoint IIQ Engineer):** policies, entitlements, SoD, LDAP, API integration, troubleshooting.
- **Aiven (Senior IAM Engineer):** HRIS-driven Joiner–Mover–Leaver, Okta, SCIM, access requests and reviews.
- **BNY (SailPoint IAM Analyst):** SoD and access governance.

## Implications for the portfolio

**Must demonstrate:** understanding of authoritative identity data, roles and entitlements, identity lifecycle, approvals, control evidence and risk assessment.

**Should demonstrate later:** reconciliations, target integrations and troubleshooting.

**Not required for MVP:** licensed SailPoint or Entra Governance, full PAM/JIT, a sophisticated UI.
